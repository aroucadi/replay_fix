# Release Notes: ReplayFix v1.9.1

**Release Tag**: `v1.9.1`  
**Release Date**: 2026-11-03  
**Architecture Theme**: Spec 190 Convergence & Hardening Patch — Benchmark Manifest Harmonization, Fast Playwright Resolution & Attached SPA Hydration Readiness  

---

## 1. Executive Summary
ReplayFix v1.9.1 is a convergence and reliability hardening release on top of v1.9.0 (Pure Application-Agnostic Verification & High-Throughput Parallel Swarm Benchmark Engine). It resolves manifest key harmonization in `ManifestParser::load_manifest`, eliminates synchronous `npm.cmd` startup stalls inside `PlaywrightBridge` by propagating ancestor `NODE_PATH` and `REPLAYFIX_PLAYWRIGHT_PATH` into ephemeral scratch sandboxes, and hardens DOM hydration readiness checks for Attached SPA Mode on Windows.

---

## 2. Key Fixes & Hardening

### A. Benchmark Sweep Manifest Resolution (`replayfix-core::sweep`)
- **`ManifestParser::load_manifest`**: Resolves the benchmark suite identifier across `"benchmark_suite"`, `"suite"`, and `"suite_name"` keys, defaulting cleanly to `"MetaPulse-20"`.
- **Suite & Tuple Verification**: Explicitly validates `(suite_name, tasks)` in `test_manifest_parser_loads_metapulse_manifest_safely` and harmonizes `crates/replayfix-cli/tests/v19_parallel_sweep.rs` with the authoritative `MetaPulse-20` suite name.

### B. Playwright Bridge Fast Resolution & Windows CDP Tolerance (`replayfix-core::browser`)
- **Two-Tier `loadPlaywright()` Resolution**: Evaluates fast synchronous filesystem candidates (`REPLAYFIX_PLAYWRIGHT_PATH`, `'playwright'`, local `node_modules`, and `LOCALAPPDATA/npm-cache`) prior to spawning fallback `npm.cmd` child processes.
- **Ancestor `node_modules` Discovery**: `PlaywrightBridge::start_with_script_and_timeouts` walks ancestor directories of `std::env::current_dir()` and `std::env::current_exe()` to inject `NODE_PATH` and `REPLAYFIX_PLAYWRIGHT_PATH` when `replayfix` is invoked from isolated scratch directories.
- **Hydration Timeout Hardening**: Increases `locator.click` timeout tolerance to `20,000ms` and default bridge startup/command timeouts to `25s` under heavy multi-worker CPU load.

### C. Attached SPA Mode Hydration Verification (`crates/replayfix-cli/tests/cli.rs`)
- **Explicit Asset Readiness Polling**: Verifies HTTP readiness of both `/` and `/public/app.js` on the external SPA server prior to launching `replayfix repro --url` in `test_attached_spa_mode_connects_to_external_url`.
